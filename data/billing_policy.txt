
CloudDesk Billing Policy

CloudDesk offers three subscription plans:

Basic: $10 per month.
Professional: $25 per month.
Enterprise: $80 per month.

Customers can cancel their subscriptions at any time.

Refunds are available within 14 days of the initial purchase.

To request a refund, contact billing@clouddesk.example.

Subscription changes take effect at the next billing cycle.
